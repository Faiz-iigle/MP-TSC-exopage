EXO

Long ago, on an exoplanet far beyond our solar system, the Tree of Life gave twelve boys twelve powers: water, light, fire, earth, teleportation, wind, frost, telekinesis, flight, healing, lightning and time. When darkness split their world in two, the twelve came to Earth as two units, EXO-K in Seoul and EXO-M in Beijing, each holding one half of the same heart.

The legend says that only when the twelve become one again will a new world awaken. This page remembers the original lineup that started the story and follows EXO all the way to today.

Meet the members

About EXO

EXO is a boy group formed by SM Entertainment. The name comes from exoplanet, a planet outside our solar system. In their debut story, all twelve members came from that planet, and each one carried a superpower.

They first stood on stage at the SBS Gayo Daejeon on December 29, 2011, and officially debuted on April 8, 2012 with MAMA. The song came out twice at once: in Korean by EXO-K and in Mandarin by EXO-M.

2013 changed everything. The album XOXO and its follow-up single Growl made EXO a household name, and XOXO became the first album in twelve years to sell over a million copies in Korea.

From twelve to nine

The twelve-member lineup did not last long. Kris filed a lawsuit against SM in May 2014, Luhan followed in October 2014, and Tao in August 2015. The Kris and Luhan cases were settled in July 2016, and Tao's contract officially ended in March 2018.

From 2015 on, EXO carried on as nine members and stopped promoting as separate units. Xiumin, Baekhyun and Chen sat out the latest album, REVERXE (2026).

Timeline

From the first teaser to the next concert.

2011

  1. December SM starts introducing the members one by one through a long series of teaser videos.
  2. December 29 First stage at the SBS Gayo Daejeon, before the official debut.

2012

  1. January 30 Prologue single What Is Love.
  2. March 9 Second prologue single History.
  3. March 31 Debut showcase in Seoul, followed by Beijing the next day.
  4. April 8 Official debut with MAMA, EXO-K in Korea and EXO-M in China on the same day.

2013

  1. June 3 First full album XOXO with the title track Wolf.
  2. August 5 XOXO repackage with Growl, the song that made EXO famous.
  3. November 22 First Album of the Year at the Mnet Asian Music Awards (MAMA).
  4. December 9 Winter album Miracles in December.
  5. December XOXO passes one million copies sold.

2014

  1. May 7 Second mini album Overdose.
  2. May 15 Kris files a lawsuit to end his contract.
  3. May 23 First solo tour, EXO FROM. EXOPLANET #1 – The Lost Planet, opens in Seoul.
  4. October 10 Luhan files a lawsuit to end his contract.

2015

  1. March 7 Second tour, EXO PLANET #2 – The EXO'luXion, opens in Seoul.
  2. March 30 Second album EXODUS with Call Me Baby.
  3. June 3 Repackage LOVE ME RIGHT.
  4. August Tao files a lawsuit to end his contract.
  5. November 4 Japanese debut with Love Me Right ~romantic universe~.
  6. December 10 Winter album Sing For You.

2016

  1. June 9 Third album EX'ACT with Lucky One and Monster.
  2. July 21 The Kris and Luhan lawsuits are settled.
  3. July 22 Third tour, EXO PLANET #3 – The EXO'rDIUM, opens in Seoul.
  4. August 18 Repackage LOTTO.
  5. October 31 First sub-unit EXO-CBX (Chen, Baekhyun, Xiumin) debuts with Hey Mama!.
  6. December 19 Winter album For Life.

2017

  1. July 18 Fourth album THE WAR with Ko Ko Bop.
  2. September 5 Repackage The Power of Music.
  3. November 24 Fourth tour, EXO PLANET #4 – The ElyXiOn, opens in Seoul.
  4. December 1 Fifth Album of the Year at MAMA in a row.
  5. December 26 Winter album Universe.

2018

  1. February 25 Performance at the closing ceremony of the PyeongChang Winter Olympics.
  2. March 15 Tao's contract with SM officially ends.
  3. November 2 Fifth album Don't Mess Up My Tempo.
  4. December 13 Repackage LOVE SHOT.

2019

  1. May 7 Xiumin is the first member to start military service.
  2. July 1 D.O. enlists.
  3. July Fifth tour, EXO PLANET #5 – EXplOration, opens in Seoul.
  4. July 22 Sub-unit EXO-SC (Sehun, Chanyeol) debuts.
  5. November 27 Sixth album OBSESSION.

2020

  1. May 14 Suho enlists.
  2. October 26 Chen enlists.
  3. December Xiumin completes his service.

2021

  1. January D.O. completes his service.
  2. March 29 Chanyeol enlists.
  3. May 6 Baekhyun enlists.
  4. June 7 Special album Don't Fight the Feeling.

2022

  1. 2022 Suho, Chen and Chanyeol complete their service.

2023

  1. February Baekhyun completes his service.
  2. May 11 Kai enlists.
  3. June Contract dispute between CBX and SM, settled on June 19.
  4. July 10 Seventh album EXIST with Cream Soda.
  5. December 21 Sehun enlists.

2025

  1. February 10 Kai completes his service.
  2. September 20 Sehun completes his service. Every member has now served.
  3. October 27 SM announces the eighth album, without Xiumin, Baekhyun and Chen.
  4. December 14 Fan meeting EXO'verse and the I'm Home music video.

2026

  1. January 19 Eighth album REVERXE with Crown.
  2. January 28 to February 1 Crown wins on all five weekly music shows.
  3. February 26 REVERXE passes one million copies.
  4. April 10 Sixth tour, EXO PLANET #6 – EXhOrizon, opens: 14 cities, 27 shows.
  5. June 6 and 7 EXhOrizon comes to Indonesia Arena, Jakarta.
  6. July 26 The tour ends.
  7. November 6 to 8 Encore concert EXhOrizon [dot] at KSPO Dome, Seoul.
